Origin Specification

FOUNDED IN COLOMBO,
SRI LANKA.

FleetNET GLOBAL emerged from a simple observation: existing fleet management solutions were fragmented, unreliable, and failed to deliver the insights modern businesses needed.

Our founders recognized that true innovation required controlling the entire technology stack—from hardware sensors to the cloud analytics platform.

Purpose

Integrated Architecture

Controlling the entire stack lets us deliver uncompromised data reliability, rapid innovation cycles, and a seamless operator experience.

Our Journey

A story told in
LAYERS.

We built FleetNET GLOBAL by listening to operators first and engineering every layer of the platform for clarity, speed, and trust.

Chapter 01

The Observation

In Colombo, we saw fleets running on disconnected tools that could not explain what was really happening on the road. We believed clarity had to be engineered into the system itself.

Chapter 02

Full-Stack Control

Our founders united automotive technology, IoT hardware, and enterprise software to control the entire stack. From sensors to analytics, every layer was designed to speak the same language.

Chapter 03

Scale & Impact

Today, FleetNET GLOBAL supports thousands of vehicles while staying committed to Sri Lankan engineering excellence and a partnership-first culture.
